DemandPulse

RTA Future Move Hackathon - Team 7 - Dubai bus network: demand forecast, capacity and reliability flags, cost-neutral swap. Hackathon prototype, not an official RTA publication.

Data period: ACTUAL observations for one day, Monday 15 Dec 2025 (Nol card taps, bus running times, fleet list). GTFS is the planned Sep-Dec 2026 timetable and is not compared with 2025 as the same period. No weekly, seasonal or event claims are made.

AI agent layer

  • Agent 1 - Demand and capacity analyst: reads one route summary row; explains capacity vs demand.
  • Agent 2 - Reliability analyst: reads delay and late-hour fields; suggests a running-time fix.
  • Agent 3 - Coordinator and guardrail: merges both views into CHANGE / WHY / MEASURABLE / GUARDRAIL CHECK and flags conflicts.

Agents see route-level summary tables only (no card or driver identifiers) and never compute numbers; every figure comes from Dataiku recipes.

Data handling and limits

  • NOL_ID and Driver_id are excluded from every analytical dataset; source datasets were not modified.
  • One observed day: intraday forecasts only. The test half contains the evening peak but not the morning peak.
  • Fleet capacity 0 treated as unknown; 99.7% of running-time events matched a bus with valid capacity.
  • 6,283 running-time events with an hour outside 0-23 were excluded; 34 of 200 routes have no running-time match.
  • Thresholds (1.0, 0.25, 5 min, 30%) are assumptions chosen for screening and can be tuned.
