Rule: in the same hour, a large bus (100 seats or more) moves from an under-used route-hour to a high-pressure one that runs a smaller bus. The donor must stay at 0.50 or less afterwards.

A calculation on one observed day, not a measured outcome. The other 15 crowded route-hours need a standby trip or on-board load data first.

This is the practical heart of the project: the network already has enough seats, just not in the right hours. Same buses, same trips, same hour. 13 of the 28 high-pressure route-hours fall below 1.0. Present it as a pilot to test, with passenger counters, not as a promise.
