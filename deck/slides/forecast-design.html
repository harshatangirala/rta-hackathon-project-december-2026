Leak caught and discarded

A first session with a random split scored MAE 11.4 and R² 0.81. Too good to be true, so we rebuilt it on the time split.

Two horizons, one direct model each. The 60-minute model may only use information at least one hour old, so lags 1 to 3 and the short moving average are switched off. Route, slot and period were rejected so the model learns from recent demand, not from memorising a route or a clock time. The story of the leaky first session shows we checked our own work: a random split lets the model peek at neighbouring slots from the same afternoon.
