HONEST LIMITS

What we do not claim

  • One observed day: no weekday, seasonal or event effects; the test is the same day’s evening
  • The morning peak was in training only, so it was not tested
  • Demand pressure is card taps per seat, not on-board load
  • The swap is a what-if, not a measured result
  • Slow segments can be traffic or an unrealistic timetable
  • The forecast does not yet feed the decision recipe; it is validated separately
  • The AI checker proves numbers and banned terms, not reasoning
  • A few Nol route codes may be marine or tram services
  • 19% of fleet records had no capacity; standing capacity is unverified
  • Sending the daily report automatically needs an admin-set messaging channel
Saying these out loud builds trust. Each limit has a next step on the following slide: more days of data, passenger counters, wiring the forecast in, nearby-stop matching and an automated daily run.
