DemandPulse

Matching Dubai bus capacity to passenger demand

Forecasts, capacity gaps, congestion hotspots and checked AI action cards, built end to end in one Dataiku Flow.

One observed day: Monday 15 Dec 2025 · Dataiku DSS 15.2 · Hackathon prototype, not an official RTA publication

Open with the one-line promise: DemandPulse shows where Dubai bus seats, delays and demand do not match, and what RTA can change without touching fares, trips or infrastructure. Everything you will see was built inside Dataiku on one real day of Nol taps and bus running times.
