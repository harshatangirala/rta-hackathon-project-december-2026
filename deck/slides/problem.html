THE PROBLEM

Seats, delays and demand do not line up

Where and when do bus capacity, reliability and passenger demand not match, and what can RTA change without raising fares, removing trips or building infrastructure?

Control room

Which corridors lose time right now, and which actions are safe to take

Service planners

Which route-hours are over- or under-supplied, and which bus swaps would help

Communications

Which nearby hour is calmer on a busy route, as a demand-shifting message

Guardrails on every recommendation: no fare change · no trip or frequency cut · no new infrastructure · driver rest and vehicle types respected · two human confirmations per action

We framed the brief as one operational question with three users. The guardrails come straight from the hackathon guide’s DON’Ts, and we applied them to every recommendation, including the AI-written ones.
